**About This Project**

This project was created to organize gameplay knowledge, tactical observations, and educational strategy material related to Stick War Legacy.

The goal is to help players better understand:

- battle pacing
- army composition
- defensive positioning
- spell efficiency
- unit counter systems
- campaign progression mechanics

Many strategy guides online focus only on basic gameplay instructions, while advanced tactical concepts are often scattered across community discussions and player forums.

This repository attempts to document some of those gameplay observations in a more structured format.

**About George Harrison**

George Harrison focuses on strategy-oriented mobile gameplay systems, tactical learning patterns, and player progression mechanics.
