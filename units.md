# Stick War Legacy Unit Strategy Analysis

## Understanding Unit Roles in Stick War Legacy

One of the most common mistakes beginners make in Stick War Legacy is building random units without understanding how army composition affects battle efficiency.

Different units serve completely different purposes during gameplay progression. Some are designed for early resource control, while others become valuable during defensive survival phases or late-game offensive pushes.

Understanding unit timing, positioning, and counter-matchups is often more important than simply producing larger armies.

This strategy page documents gameplay observations, tactical patterns, and practical unit usage scenarios commonly seen during campaign progression and survival battles.

---

## Miner Strategy

### Role
Economy & resource generation

Miners are the foundation of every successful battle setup because gold production directly controls army growth speed.

Many aggressive beginner players underinvest in miners early, which often causes weak mid-game scaling and poor defensive recovery.

---

### Effective Miner Strategies

- Maintain stable early gold production
- Avoid overproducing combat units too early
- Protect miners during enemy pressure
- Expand economy before large offensive pushes

During longer battles, strong economy management often becomes more important than aggressive early attacks.

---

### Common Beginner Mistakes

- Sending miners too close to enemy pressure
- Ignoring economy upgrades
- Producing expensive units too early
- Neglecting defensive positioning

---

## Swordwrath Analysis

### Role
Fast melee offense

Swordwrath units are commonly used for early-game pressure due to their mobility and low deployment cost.

They perform well during:
- early aggressive pushes
- resource harassment
- fast counterattacks
- quick battlefield reinforcement

However, poorly timed Swordwrath attacks often fail against defensive ranged setups.

---

### Tactical Observations

Swordwrath units become significantly stronger when:
- enemy archers remain unprotected
- economy advantage already exists
- attack timing is coordinated properly

Many advanced players use Swordwrath pressure mainly to disrupt enemy economy rather than fully end battles immediately.

---

### Weaknesses

Swordwrath units struggle against:
- heavy defensive formations
- giant units
- concentrated ranged damage
- defensive spell combinations

---

## Archer Strategy

### Role
Defensive ranged support

Archers are among the most efficient defensive units in Stick War Legacy because they can apply constant pressure while remaining protected behind frontline units.

They are especially effective during:
- defensive survival
- choke-point battles
- slow enemy pushes
- late-game army scaling

---

### Effective Positioning

Archers perform best when:
- protected behind melee units
- grouped efficiently
- combined with economy stability
- supported by defensive spells

Poor positioning often causes archers to collapse quickly against aggressive enemy pushes.

---

## Spearton Analysis

### Role
Defensive frontline control

Speartons provide strong defensive stability and are frequently used to absorb damage while ranged units attack safely from behind.

Their defensive utility becomes especially important during:
- survival mode
- giant encounters
- heavy melee pressure
- late campaign battles

---

### Strategic Advantages

Speartons help:
- slow aggressive enemy pushes
- protect ranged units
- create defensive spacing
- stabilize weak economy phases

Many players underestimate how valuable defensive spacing becomes during difficult campaign stages.

---

## Magikill Strategy

### Role
Area damage & battlefield disruption

Magikill units become dangerous when battles grow larger and enemy formations become more concentrated.

Their attack patterns can heavily punish:
- grouped melee armies
- defensive clustering
- slow advancing units

However, they require proper protection because of their high cost and vulnerability during repositioning.

---

### Best Usage Timing

Magikill units perform best:
- during mid-to-late game scaling
- behind defensive frontlines
- when economy is already stabilized
- against grouped enemy formations

---

## Giant Unit Analysis

### Role
Late-game offensive pressure

Giants are primarily used for:
- frontline destruction
- defensive breakthroughs
- high-pressure late-game attacks

Although powerful, Giants can become inefficient if deployed too early without stable gold production.

---

### Common Tactical Mistakes

Many players:
- rush Giants too early
- ignore supporting units
- weaken economy excessively
- lose map control while saving resources

Balanced army composition usually performs better than relying entirely on expensive units.

---

## Unit Combination Strategies

Successful battles usually depend more on balanced unit combinations than individual unit strength.

Examples of effective combinations include:

| Combination | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Spearton + Archers | Defensive scaling |
| Swordwrath + Archers | Fast pressure |
| Giants + Archers | Late-game push |
| Spearton + Magikill | Defensive control |
| Miner-heavy economy + ranged defense | Long survival battles |

---

## Tactical Learning Patterns

Many advanced players gradually shift from:
- aggressive unit spam
to:
- controlled economy management
- timing-based attacks
- defensive positioning
- efficient counter-unit deployment

This transition often improves campaign consistency significantly.
