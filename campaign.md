# Stick War Legacy Campaign Progression Guide

## Understanding Campaign Progression

Campaign progression in Stick War Legacy becomes significantly more difficult as players move into later stages where enemy pressure, economy management, defensive positioning, and unit coordination all become increasingly important.

Many beginner players struggle because they focus entirely on attacking quickly instead of learning:
- economy timing
- defensive recovery
- battlefield control
- efficient unit scaling
- counter-unit positioning

This strategy archive documents gameplay observations and tactical progression patterns commonly seen throughout campaign mode.

---

## Early Campaign Strategy

### Focus on Economy Stability

During early campaign stages, stable gold generation usually matters more than aggressive attacks.

Players who:
- overproduce units too early
- neglect miners
- rush expensive units
often struggle later when battles become longer and more demanding.

---

### Recommended Early Priorities

Early progression becomes more consistent when players:
- build stable miner economy
- defend efficiently
- avoid unnecessary losses
- expand army gradually
- protect ranged units carefully

Many successful campaign runs begin with controlled defensive scaling rather than constant aggression.

---

## Mid-Game Campaign Progression

As campaign difficulty increases, battles begin introducing:
- larger enemy waves
- stronger defensive formations
- ranged pressure
- heavy frontline units
- economy disruption

At this stage, tactical positioning becomes increasingly important.

---

### Mid-Game Tactical Adjustments

Players often improve campaign consistency by:
- balancing offense and defense
- improving ranged protection
- avoiding reckless attacks
- coordinating spell timing
- adapting army composition to enemy pressure

Many failed battles occur because players continue using early-game strategies against stronger defensive setups.

---

## Defensive Scaling Strategies

One of the most important progression concepts in Stick War Legacy is learning when to slow the pace of battle and stabilize economy before launching large attacks.

Defensive scaling helps:
- preserve resources
- recover from failed pushes
- protect ranged units
- prepare stronger late-game armies

Players who constantly attack without economy stability often lose long-term battlefield control.

---

## Campaign Unit Priorities

Different campaign stages favor different army compositions.

| Campaign Situation | Effective Tactical Focus |
|---|---|
| Early aggressive enemy pressure | Defensive economy scaling |
| Heavy melee enemy waves | Ranged support protection |
| Defensive enemy formations | Controlled frontline pressure |
| Long survival battles | Economy stability |
| Late-game scaling fights | Balanced army composition |

---

## Common Campaign Mistakes

Many players repeatedly struggle because they:
- rush expensive units too early
- ignore economy development
- neglect defensive positioning
- overcommit during failed attacks
- waste spells during low-pressure moments
- expose ranged units unnecessarily

Campaign progression becomes significantly easier once players improve defensive discipline and economy timing.

---

## Understanding Battle Momentum

Many advanced campaign battles are decided by:
- economy efficiency
- defensive recovery
- resource preservation
- army scaling speed
- positioning control

Battle momentum often changes gradually rather than instantly.

Players who panic during temporary pressure frequently make poor tactical decisions that weaken long-term battlefield stability.

---

## Spell Timing During Campaign Progression

Spell management becomes increasingly important during difficult campaign stages.

Efficient spell timing can:
- stabilize weak defenses
- protect important units
- recover battlefield control
- slow enemy momentum
- support offensive pushes

However, excessive spell usage without stable economy often creates long-term scaling problems.

---

## Late Campaign Strategy

Later campaign stages usually require:
- stronger defensive spacing
- better ranged protection
- coordinated attacks
- balanced resource management
- controlled battlefield pacing

Aggressive early-game tactics alone often become unreliable during advanced progression stages.

---

### Late-Game Tactical Patterns

Experienced players frequently:
- slow down battle pacing
- stabilize economy before attacking
- protect ranged formations carefully
- maintain balanced unit production
- avoid unnecessary frontline losses

This creates more consistent long-term progression during difficult battles.

---

## Survival & Endurance Learning

Campaign progression also helps players improve:
- defensive patience
- tactical adaptation
- resource management discipline
- recovery decision-making
- army positioning awareness

Many players notice large improvement after learning how to survive pressure instead of forcing constant attacks.
